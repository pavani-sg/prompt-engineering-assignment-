## Prompt 1 – Explain a Concept

### Prompt
Explain Artificial Intelligence in simple words for a first-year engineering student. Give 3 real-life examples.

### Output
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. AI can learn from data, recognize patterns, solve problems and make decisions.

Three real-life examples are:
1. Voice assistants such as Siri and Google Assistant.
2. Recommendation systems on YouTube and Netflix.
3. Face recognition on smartphones.

## Prompt 6 – Comparison

### Prompt
Compare Human Intelligence and Machine Intelligence using five differences in a simple table.

### Output

| Human Intelligence | Machine Intelligence |
|---|---|
| Learns from experiences | Learns from data and training |
| Uses emotions and intuition | Uses algorithms and patterns |
| Can adapt creatively to unfamiliar situations | Usually works within its trained capabilities |
| Understands emotions and social situations | Has limited understanding of emotions |
| Biological | Artificial and computer-based |

## Prompt 9 – Step-by-Step Explanation

### Prompt
Explain how a search engine works in 6 simple steps that a beginner can understand.

### Output
1. The user enters a search query.
2. The search engine looks through its stored information.
3. It identifies web pages related to the query.
4. Algorithms rank the pages according to relevance.
5. The most relevant results are displayed.
6. The user selects a result to find the required information.

## Prompt 10 – Improving a Prompt

### Prompt
Improve this vague prompt: “Tell me about AI.” Make it more specific and useful for a college presentation.

### Output
**Improved Prompt:**

“Explain Artificial Intelligence for a first-year engineering college presentation. Cover its definition, how it works, three real-world applications, advantages, limitations and future scope. Use simple language and organize the answer with clear headings and bullet points.”

**Why the improved prompt is better:**

The improved prompt clearly specifies the audience, purpose, topics to cover and desired format. This helps the AI generate a more relevant and organized response.
